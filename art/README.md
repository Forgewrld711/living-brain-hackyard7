# living brain — art direction

sketches for twelve to animate. the graph is a creature, not a diagram. same body in every mood: a little night-thing made of glowing nodes and thin irregular threads, with a faint indigo membrane holding the silhouette together. two slightly larger gold nodes in the head are its eyes. no faces, no mouths, no ui, no logos, no type.

a faint moon sits in the upper right in every frame. lots of empty night around it. the creature should always feel small in the dark, even when it's well fed.

## palette

- night / ink ground: `#0B0C12`
- lifted night around the moon: `#1A1E2A`
- moon glow: `#C9D0DC`
- healthy node core (moonlight gold): `#F4E6C1`
- healthy node mid: `#E8C97A`
- node halo / cool silver-teal: `#A8C5D4`
- threads: `#C9D4E8`, dimmer `#8FA3C4`
- membrane (very faint): `#2A3348`
- neglected / hungry nodes: `#6B7280` down to ashy `#3D4658`, almost out at `#2E3440`
- arguing, warm flare: `#E8A87C` into `#E07A4A`
- arguing, cool flare: `#7B9CFF` into `#8B6BFF`

keep the gold soft. this is moonlight, not neon.

## shapes

- nodes are soft circles with a warm core and a cooler halo. never hard dots, never icons.
- threads are thin and a little hand-drawn, slightly irregular, like hair or spider silk. not straight ui edges.
- the head is a loose ring of nodes. the torso is a dewdrop cluster inside that faint membrane.
- limbs are thread-chains that end in a single node, or, when a topic gets dense, a small flower-like bud of nodes.
- unconfirmed links (kiln's rule) can be even fainter and slightly dotted. confirmed ones light up and stay.

## how each mood reads

**hatchling** (`01-hatchling.jpg`)
tiny, curious, barely there. a handful of nodes, two or three short stubby limbs, modest glow. it should feel like it just woke up in a big night. occupy only the center of the frame.

**well fed** (`02-well-fed.jpg`)
same creature, grown. denser clusters, brighter gold, five or six limbs reaching out, new buds opening at the tips where notes have piled up. open, rounded, proud without being loud. this is "that one's getting heavy. want me to grow a new limb for it?"

**hungry** (`03-hungry.jpg`)
still the grown body, still softly lit — hunger is not shrinking, and it's not dying. one limb only goes neglected: it droops, the thread thins, the nodes fade to cool ash. the rest of the creature leans toward that limb, like it noticed. invitation, not guilt. "haven't tasted this in a while. still thinking about it?"

**arguing** (`04-arguing.jpg`)
two nodes flare in clashing colors, one warm coral, one cool violet, with the threads between them pulled taut and twitching. the body tenses a little; the other limbs may bristle or recoil. the eye-nodes stay gold. this is a startled disagreement, not a fight and not an explosion. "you said this. you also said that. which one's true now?"

## notes for animating

- let the whole creature breathe: a slow pulse in the node glow, a slight drift in the membrane, maybe two or three seconds per breath.
- growth should feel like budding, not popping. new nodes swell out of a cluster; a new limb unspools from a dense patch.
- neglect is gravity. the hungry limb sags over time and cools toward ash. when that topic is fed again, the limb lifts and the gold comes back from the core outward.
- the lean toward a hungry limb is a gentle attend, a tilt of the head-cluster, not a collapse.
- contradiction is a twitch: a short vibration in the tense threads, a flicker in the two flaring nodes, then they hold until the human answers. if the old note fades (engram's rule), its flare cools and the thread loosens; it is not deleted.
- keep motion quiet. no bounce, no cartoon squash, no ui easing that looks like a settings panel.
- hatchling stays small in the frame and grows into the space as it's fed, so the night around it always matters.
