ready to paste. remix of [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md)'s graph view.

## one line

Obsidian's graph view, except your second brain is alive.

## short pitch

You talk or text to it. Your notes become a creature: it suggests links and only lights them when you say yes, grows new limbs where your thinking is dense, gets hungry for topics you've left alone, and argues when two notes disagree. It never deletes an old note. It just lets it fade.

The look is our own, not a visual clone of Obsidian. The starter graph is thirty-six cities and the threads that tie them, from waystone's street-rules map.
